# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-09-03

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Private Bali ATV, river, and village cycling adventures based in Pejeng near Ubud — book on WhatsApp with no upfront payment.
**What it does:** Sekar Bali Activity sells all-inclusive adventure packages (Single/Tandem ATV at All New Bali Adventure, optional Wos River tubing, whitewater rafting, canyon tubing, Ubud ricefield cycling, partner cooking class) with transparent IDR tier pricing and optional hotel pickup.
**Product category:** Bali adventure tours / Ubud ATV & outdoor activities
**Product type:** Local tour operator / service business
**Business model:** Direct WhatsApp booking; pay on confirm/experience; packages from ~IDR 359K–859K; group tier discounts; pickup add-ons

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** N/A (B2C travelers)
**Decision-makers:** Couples, families, and small friend groups staying in Ubud (and nearby) planning 1–2 adventure days
**Primary use case:** Book a private or small-group ATV / rafting / cycling day without opaque aggregator pricing
**Jobs to be done:**
- Experience a thrilling ATV jungle ride safely as a beginner
- Combine land + water (ATV + tubing) in one day
- Do an authentic Pejeng village cycling + culture day with meals and free Ubud pickup
**Use cases:**
- Honeymoon / couple tandem ATV
- Family half-day rafting or tubing
- Full-day ricefield cycling with free hotel pickup
- Cycling + evening Balinese cooking class combo

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Booking Bali adventures feels opaque — unclear arena location, hidden pickup fees, mass-group vibes, and hard-to-compare package prices.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Aggregators (GetYourGuide/Klook) bury inclusions and local operator identity
- Big ATV operators feel crowded / impersonal
- Conflicting addresses confuse self-drive guests
**What it costs them:** Wasted half-days, Grab surprises, wrong meeting point, disappointment vs “authentic Bali”
**Emotional tension:** FOMO + fear of tourist traps; anxiety about safety and “will WhatsApp booking actually work?”

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local Ubud ATV operators / All New Bali Adventure-adjacent sellers — falls short when packaging + WhatsApp clarity + Pejeng storytelling is weak
**Secondary:** Aggregator listings — falls short on personalization, transparent pickup rules, and local brand trust
**Indirect:** DIY Grab + random roadside rentals — falls short on safety briefing, insurance, and guided experience

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Pejeng-based local family operator (not a faceless marketplace listing)
- Clear ATV arena: All New Bali Adventure + labeled corporate / meeting / activity-base addresses
- Transparent tier pricing + free Ubud pickup on cycling only (stated upfront)
- WhatsApp-first booking with no upfront payment to inquire
**How we do it differently:** One site for packages, pickup rules, location roles, and ready WhatsApp message
**Why that's better:** Less confusion, faster confirmation, trust before payment
**Why customers choose us:** Local Pejeng authenticity + all-inclusive ATV/cycling packages + easy WhatsApp

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “Is WhatsApp booking legit?” | No upfront payment to inquire; confirm date/price first; corporate office matches Google Business Profile |
| “Where do I meet for ATV?” | Activity base: All New Bali Adventure in Pejeng — or book hotel pickup |
| “Is it beginner-friendly?” | Full safety briefing, gear, English-speaking guide; no experience required |

**Anti-persona:** Travelers wanting ultra-cheap unguided rentals, large party-bus group tours, or same-day extreme Class IV+ whitewater

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Crowded tours, unclear fees, tourist-trap rice terraces
**Pull:** Private/small-group Pejeng experiences, transparent IDR, WhatsApp ease
**Habit:** Defaulting to first Klook/GYG result
**Anxiety:** Self-driving to wrong pin; payment risk; language barrier
