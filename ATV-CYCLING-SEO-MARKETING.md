## 4) SEO marketing pillars (90 days)

### Pillar A — Money pages (Acquisition)
Ship SERP-ready titles/metas/FAQs that mirror query language and inclusions. Keep schema Offer prices accurate.

### Pillar B — Spoke blogs (Acquisition → money)
Each commercial query cluster gets **one** hub post that answers the question in 1,200–1,500+ words with operator-first detail, then links to the tour + `/book`.

### Pillar C — Differentiation content (Shareable + Search)
- Private vs mass ATV  
- Pejeng vs Tegallalang cycling  
- Honest “is it worth it” posts  

### Pillar D — Conversion marketing (Activation)
- WhatsApp prefilled CTAs with price + hotel fields  
- Homepage + blog CTAs: “Free to inquire — no deposit”  
- Inclusion bullets above the fold: lunch / gear / insurance / free Ubud pickup (cycling only)

### Pillar E — What we will not do
- Bid or publish for Dragon Cave / Kuber tunnel / Goa Naga as if we run them  
- Mass thin blogs on dress code / airport (already steal impressions without bookings)  
- Fake AggregateRating

## 5) Ready-to-use marketing copy

### ATV — ads / WhatsApp / social hooks
1. **Jungle ATV near Ubud from IDR 600K** — lunch, boots, helmet, insurance. Beginners welcome.  
2. **Single or tandem** at All New Bali Adventure. Optional **Wos River tubing** after the track.  
3. **WhatsApp booking, no deposit** — send name, date, hotel, guest count.  
4. Not a 40-person bus tour: small-group pacing, clear IDR tiers.

### Cycling — ads / WhatsApp / social hooks
1. **Rice paddy cycling in Pejeng** — quieter than Tegallalang photo strips.  
2. **IDR 475K** with **lunch + free Ubud hotel pickup**.  
3. Flat village / Subak paths — couples, families, culture travelers.  
4. Add evening **Balinese cooking class** for a full culture day.

### Email / CRM one-liners
- “ATV morning, optional tubing afternoon — still back for sunset in Ubud.”  
- “Skip the terrace selfie traffic: Pejeng Subak lanes + village lunch.”
