**Our ATV gap vs Kuber:** They own “cave / tunnel / waterfall ride.” We own **Sedang mud + river + lunch + WhatsApp IDR**. Do not invent a cave. Commercial spoke: `/blog/bali-atv-vs-kuber-cave-2026`.
