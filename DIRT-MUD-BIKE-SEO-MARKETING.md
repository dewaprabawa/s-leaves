**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Companion to:** `ATV-CYCLING-SEO-MARKETING.md`  
**Site products:**
- `/tours/bali-atv-adventure` — 4-wheel ATV / quad (All New Bali Adventure; mud-jungle trails)
- `/tours/bali-dirt-bike-adventure` — 2-wheel dirt / enduro / motocross (Tabanan + Kintamani / volcano)

---

## 1) Critical taxonomy (do not mix these in SEO)

| Traveler says | What they usually mean | Our product | Money URL |
|---------------|------------------------|-------------|-----------|
| **ATV / quad bike / muddy ATV / sport ATV mud** | 4 wheels, stable, beginner OK | ATV at All New Bali Adventure | `/tours/bali-atv-adventure` |
| **Dirt bike / enduro / motocross / trail bike / KLX / CRF / KTM** | 2 wheels, clutch/balance, higher skill | Dirt bike packages | `/tours/bali-dirt-bike-adventure` |
| **Mud bike** (ambiguous) | Often muddy ATV *or* dirt bike in mud | Clarify in copy: “muddy ATV” vs “dirt bike mud trails” | Route by intent |
| **Sport ATV** | Marketing synonym for adventure ATV | ATV page (not dirt bike) | ATV URL |

**Rule:** Never title the dirt-bike page as “ATV,” and never sell dirt bike as “beginner family ATV.” Cross-link with a chooser FAQ instead.

### Market price anchors (competitors, 2026)

Specialist operators (Bali Dirt Bikes, TrailX, local Tabanan/enduro shops) typically list:
- Entry KLX/CRF half day ~ **IDR 1.2M–1.8M**
- Full day / bigger bikes ~ **IDR 1.8M–5M+**
- Multi-day enduro much higher

Our page base **IDR 1,200,000** with tiered bike options aligns with entry half-day Tabanan — marketing should show **bike tier + half/full + route**, not a single vague price.

## 8) Do / Don’t

**Do**
- Separate 2-wheel vs 4-wheel in nav, schema, and ads
- Lead dirt bike with **route + bike model + skill**
- Use “muddy ATV / mud track” on the ATV page only
- Keep license / age / min 2 riders transparent

**Don’t**
- Rank dirt bike for `ATV Ubud` (wrong intent, poor conversion)
- Promise “no experience needed” on dirt bike
- Invent cave/tunnel ATV features on either page
- Publish thin “mud bike” doorway pages
